The Kalima Story

Bringing The Soul of Kerala Kitchen to Chennai

At Kalima Kerala Kitchen Hotel, food is more than a meal—it is a celebration of Kerala's lush coastlines, fragrant spice plantations, and centuries-old culinary heritage.

Every morning, our master chefs source fresh ingredients, cold-pressed coconut oil, and hand-pounded spices to prepare soulful delicacies. From the slow-cooked fragrance of Thalassery Kaima Dum Biryani to the irresistible layers of beaten Malabar Porotta and banana-leaf roasted Karimeen, every bite is a journey home.
